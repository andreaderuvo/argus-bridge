argus-sync — a folder of an Argus server, kept in sync with a folder on this computer

  argus-sync connect '<link from Argus>' <folder here>
      In Argus: a folder's menu → Sync to your computer… gives the link.
  argus-sync list                 the folders connected
  argus-sync run                  keep them all in sync, until stopped (Ctrl-C)
  argus-sync once                 one round, then exit
  argus-sync fetch <name> <path>  bring a file over the size limit
  argus-sync pause|resume <name>
  argus-sync forget <name>        stop syncing it (the files stay, on both sides)
  argus-sync computer ['<link from Argus>'] [--browser] [--terminal] [--actions] [--nothing]
      Connect this computer to Argus, until disconnected there. What it lends is what you switch on,
      and nothing until you do: --browser (a Chrome of its own, for the server's agents), --terminal
      (a shell here, in a window of Argus), --actions (commands agents ask, each allowed by you in
      Argus). Remembered: later, `argus-sync computer` alone. --headless, --chrome <path> for the
      browser; --forget to forget the connection here.
      In Argus: Settings → Computers → Connect this computer gives the link.
